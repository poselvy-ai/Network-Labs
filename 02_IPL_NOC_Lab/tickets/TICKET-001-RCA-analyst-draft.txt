TICKET-001
Date: 11/18/26
Start Time: 15:22 PST
End Time: 15:34 PST
Severity (SEV-1 to SEV-5): SEV-2
Systems Impacted: SCADA-PC1
Business Impact Summary: IPL lost control of the SCADA station on substation 1, affecting ability to monitor the electrical grid. 

15:20 SolarWinds detects SCADA-PC1 (10.2.10.10). ICMP polls failing, 3 consecutive. Site: Substation 1.
15:22 NOC Receives call from Substation 1 informing us they lost telemetry to the site. 
15:29 NOC Analysis Selvy creates ticket engages with Ops Leads, engages customer, and start to investigate
15:29 NOC Leads Escalate the ticket to SEV-2 and engaged management. 
15:32 NOC Analysis discovers SUB-R1 E0/1 administratively downed
15:34 NOC Analysis administratively UP SUB-R1 E0/1
15:35 NOC Analysis verifies from CORE_R2 can reach SCADA_PC1
15:44 Customer confirms the restoration of service
15:45 Ops Leads confirm restoration



Impact Analysis: 

User Impact:
User were not able to use the SCADA system to monitor the health of the substation 1. 

System Impact:
The logs showed that we are not able to monitor who made the change or when the change was made effectively. 

Business Impact:
IPL was at risk of financial, government regulatory, and safety impact while the port was shut down. 



Root Cause:
SUB-R1 E0/1 administratively downed preventing SCADA-PC1 to communicate

Contributing Factors:
No authentication to SUB-R1 to determine who the changed the port to down. 

Detection & Monitoring:

The incident was first detected by SolarWinds at 15:20 prior to the call at 15:22 by the customer. The TICKET was created at 15:24 per the ticket which is within standards. No required improvement at this time. 

Response:

Technician was responded well to the ticket, but did not properly categorize the severity. Ops leads did escalate correctly bringing the ticket within standard. No bridge was used for this ticket. 

Correction Action:
Enable the SUB-R1 E0/1 

Preventive Measure
Enable Security Authorization on all switches and routers. 
Sync time with NTP service.
